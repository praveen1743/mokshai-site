# Product lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The repo site already passes the one-sentence test: "AI agents that take the routine work out of your queue." A visitor knows what is offered and to whom.
- "AI plus human" is a clear product idea, the same in the CEO video and on the site.
- Five calculators give a useful first five minutes.
- Craft where nobody checks: no invented proof, labelled example inputs.
- Founder video, articles and the agents built so far all point at the same buyers: manufacturing/fabrication and MSPs.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Losing the one thing.** "AI help for everyone" is not a sentence a buyer can repeat, and it rules nothing out. The Product lens asks what to cut; this change trades a specific promise for a general one, against competitors who are mostly general already and all show proof Moksh AI does not have yet.
2. **Seams between promise and content.** A generic homepage sits on top of 18 articles, five calculators, a CEO video and a YouTube title that are almost all MSP or manufacturing. The "everyone" visitor clicks once and lands in the wrong place. A "We support" menu hides the parts that work best. With Moksh Tech already selling MSP outsourcing, a vague "We support: MSPs" item may also confuse buyers about which Moksh company to call.
3. **The live site is the product today, and it is broken.** Template pricing ($50/$80/$150 for "financial management"), a template testimonial, unsourced stats and a /pricing/ 404. No positioning survives a first visit that shows another business's content.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what does a visitor outside MSPs and manufacturing get here that the reviewed competitors do not offer?
- Walk me through that visitor's first five minutes. Which page, calculator or demo exists for them today?
- How many enquiries so far came from outside MSPs and manufacturing? (Unknown; the decision depends on it.)
- Which agents could you demo tomorrow, and for which segment?
- Where is a buyer handed between Moksh AI and the sister companies, and who owns that handover?
- If you kept only one of the four services, which would it be?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Ship the repo site first, so the template content, unsourced stats and 404 are gone before any repositioning.
2. Keep a specific one-sentence homepage promise. Broader is fine if it names an outcome (routine work off a team's plate), not "AI for everyone".
3. List a segment under "We support" only once it has a demo, a calculator or two articles behind it. At launch: MSPs and manufacturing/fabrication.
4. Give every segment page one next step (demo or calculator) into the same booking flow, with no dead ends.
5. Count enquiries by segment over a set period and decide on broadening from that count.
