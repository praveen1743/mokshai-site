You are reviewing a business decision. You cannot see the conversation this came from; everything you need is here. Facts are labelled with where they came from.

## The idea (from founder/idea.md)

- **Business:** Moksh AI, the AI practice of Moksh Infotech Pvt Ltd (Moksh Group). Builds and runs AI agents and automation with humans supervising ("AI plus human").
- **Proposed change (the founder's words, lightly edited):** "The motive of creating this website is not only for MSPs; it is for all people out there who need AI help, a generic AI site that all people have. Move MSPs, operations, etc. to a 'We support' tab. Suggest what more can be done." The founder also asked for a proper plan based on competitor websites.
- **Services:** AI strategy & consulting; custom AI agents & development; implementation & training; AI cost optimisation.
- **Pricing model:** fixed-fee discovery → fixed-scope pilot → monthly scale-and-run. Quoted per scope. No figures published.
- **Locations:** US contracting office in New Jersey; delivery team in Ahmedabad, India.
- **Sister companies in the group:** Moksh Tech (MSP outsourcing, dedicated engineers), Moksh CAD, Moksh Estimating (construction), Moksh Signs, Moksh Digital (marketing), Moksh Recruiting, Moksh Accountants.
- **Budget:** not stated. **Proof:** no published case studies, client logos or testimonials yet; the site's rules forbid invented ones.
