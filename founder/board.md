# Board summary: repositioning Moksh AI as "AI help for everyone"

**Vote: 2 PASS, 1 FUND IF. Average score 3.3 / 10.**

> **Founder decision after the vote (13 Nov 2026):** go global and open to all. The board's risks stand as written; `site-plan.md` now carries out the founder's decision and uses the conditions below to limit those risks.

| Lens | Score | Vote |
|---|---|---|
| Offers | 3 | PASS |
| Monopoly | 3 | PASS |
| Product | 4 | FUND IF |

The board rejects the move as pitched. All three members accept a "We support" menu as navigation. What they reject is replacing a specific homepage promise with a generic one.

## Risks raised by more than one member (most serious first)

1. **"Everyone" is not a market, a sentence or a beachhead** (all three). It has no single pain, budget or channel. It can't be repeated by a buyer, and it rules nothing out.
2. **Generic means losing on proof** (all three). Every generalist competitor reviewed shows proof: Morningside's logos and 48+ engagements, RaftLabs' 54 client stories, Leanware's Clutch 5.0, Botsify's 150,000 users. Moksh AI shows none yet. A specialist can win on knowing the buyer's tools. A generalist only wins on proof.
3. **It hides the only real advantages** (Monopoly, Offers, Product). Those are 15 years in manufacturing and fabrication, agents already built for those buyers, and sister companies that already sell to MSPs, estimators and accountants. Under a "We support" dropdown they become a footnote.
4. **The messages already disagree, and the content won't match** (Monopoly, Product). The CEO video says manufacturing, the YouTube title says MSPs, and the request says everyone. The 18 articles and 5 calculators are almost all MSP or operations. A generic visitor clicks once and lands somewhere that isn't for them.
5. **Price fight** (Offers, echoed by Monopoly on defensibility). With no proof and no published price, "cheaper" becomes the only difference.

## Where the members disagree

- **Offers and Monopoly** want one named niche in the hero: manufacturing/fabrication or MSPs.
- **Product** accepts a broader hero if it names an outcome ("routine work off a team's plate"), not an audience. That is closer to what the founder wants, and it is the line this summary adopts below.
- **Product alone** treats the live site as the first emergency. It currently shows template pricing for "financial management", an "Advisora Template" testimonial, finance stats and a /pricing/ 404. No positioning survives that.

## Conditions checklist

Positioning
- [ ] Homepage keeps a specific one-sentence promise naming an outcome. Not "AI for everyone". (Product, Offers, Monopoly)
- [ ] One lead agent with one demo and one real result the company can show. (Monopoly, Offers) *Open: which agent exists and can be demoed today?*
- [ ] "We support" is a secondary list. A segment appears only once it has a demo, a calculator or two articles behind it. At launch: MSPs, Manufacturing & fabrication. (all three)
- [ ] A wider audience is added only after the lead segments have published proof. (Monopoly)

Offer
- [ ] Name the stack as one offer: free audit/scorecard → fixed-fee discovery → pilot → monthly run. (Offers)
- [ ] Publish a starting price or range for discovery. (Offers) *Open: `/founder-pricing`*
- [ ] Guarantee the pilot: a defined outcome by a defined date, with a stated remedy if missed. (Offers) *Open: `/founder-offer`*

Site and funnel
- [ ] Replace the live site with the repo build first, so template pricing, the fake testimonial, unsourced stats and the 404 are gone. (Product)
- [ ] Every segment page has one next step (demo or calculator) into the same booking flow. (Product)
- [ ] Decide who owns the hand-off between Moksh AI and Moksh Tech for MSP buyers, so a buyer knows which company to call. (Product)

Measurement
- [ ] Count enquiries by segment, and by source (site vs sister-company referral), for a fixed period. Decide on broadening from that count. (Product, Monopoly) *Open: `/founder-cfo`*
- [ ] A named plan routes sister-company customers to Moksh AI, with cost per lead tracked. (Monopoly)

## The strongest version the board can see

This differs from the pitch. The strongest Moksh AI is not "AI help for everyone". It is **AI agents for businesses that run on repeatable process, built into the systems they already use and checked by a person before anything goes out.** That sentence is broad enough to welcome a dental office, an accounting firm or a logistics coordinator, which gives the founder the breadth they want. It is also specific enough to repeat, and it carries the CEO's own "AI plus human" and "you don't have to change anything".

The "We support" menu becomes the proof shelf. It opens with the two segments the company can show today: manufacturing & fabrication (the 15-year history) and MSPs (the existing content, plus the Moksh Tech relationship). Group-adjacent segments (construction estimating, accounting practices, recruiting firms) are added one at a time as each gets a demo and a result. Anyone who doesn't see their industry gets one universal entry point: the AI readiness scorecard and a fixed-price discovery. "Not sure where AI fits? Start here" serves the "everyone" visitor without promising them a homepage built for them.
